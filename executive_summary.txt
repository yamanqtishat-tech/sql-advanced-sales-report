EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The sales analysis was conducted using the store database to identify sales performance by month, product category, and city.

The total revenue generated during the analyzed period was 11,450, with a total sales quantity of 82 units. The available dataset contains sales transactions from January 2026 only; therefore, a long-term monthly trend cannot be determined from the current data.

Among the product categories, Computers generated the highest revenue with 4,800, followed by Accessories with 3,400 and Office Equipment with 3,250.

Amman generated the highest total revenue among the cities, with 5,975, followed by Irbid with 3,025 and Zarqa with 2,450.

The highest-revenue product in Amman and Irbid was the Laptop, while the Monitor generated the highest product revenue in Zarqa.

The average sale value per transaction was highest for the Computers category at 1,600, followed by Office Equipment at 812.5 and Accessories at 425.

Overall, the analysis provides a clear view of the current sales distribution across categories and cities. However, additional monthly data would be required to identify reliable sales trends over time.
